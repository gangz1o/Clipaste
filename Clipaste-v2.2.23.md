## 本次更新

本版修复右键菜单「新增分组…」无效的问题，并新增是否记录其他设备通用剪贴板内容的选项。

- **右键新增分组**：在记录上右键选择「加入分组 → 新增分组…」，会弹出分组编辑框（名称 + 图标）；创建后当前记录自动加入新分组。支持 Esc 取消，输入时不会触发面板的打字搜索。
- **其他设备的内容**：新增「通用 → 记录来自其他设备的内容」开关（默认开启）。关闭后，通过通用剪贴板从 iPhone、iPad 或其他 Mac 同步过来的内容（例如 iPhone 截图后选择「拷贝并删除」）不再被记录，也不会播放提示音。

## What's New

This release fixes the "New Group…" context menu entry and adds an option for Universal Clipboard content from other devices.

- **New group from the context menu**: "Add to Group → New Group…" now opens the group editor (name + icon). The new group is created and the record is added to it. Press Esc to cancel; typing no longer triggers type-to-search while the editor is open.
- **Content from other devices**: New "General → Record Content from Other Devices" toggle (on by default). Turn it off to skip content synced from iPhone, iPad or other Macs via Universal Clipboard, such as an iPhone screenshot saved with "Copy and Delete". Skipped content also plays no sound.
