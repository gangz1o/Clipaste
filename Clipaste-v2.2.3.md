## ✨ 本次更新

### 📌 新增贴图功能

- 支持将剪贴板内容以“贴图”形式悬浮在屏幕上，方便随时对照参考。
- 该功能默认关闭，请前往「高级 - 界面 - 贴图」手动开启。
- 开启后，拖动来源图标即可将内容随意贴在屏幕任意位置，打造你的专属桌面便签。

### ☁️ iCloud 同步提速

- 优化 iCloud 同步速度，大幅减少多端数据同步的等待时间。
- 提升同步响应效率，让剪贴板历史在不同设备间流转更迅速、更及时。

### 📱 移动端新版本发布

- iOS 新版本已上架，带来更流畅的使用体验。
- 针对移动端深度优化了 iCloud 同步速度与同步策略，弱网环境下表现更稳定。
- 与 Mac 端协同更紧密，确保跨设备剪贴板内容实时一致。

## ✨ What's New

### 📌 Pin to Screen

- You can now pin clipboard items as floating overlays on your screen for easy reference.
- This feature is disabled by default. Enable it via Advanced → Interface → Pin to Screen.
- Once enabled, simply drag the source icon to place content anywhere on your screen.

### ☁️ Faster iCloud Sync

- Improved iCloud sync speed to significantly reduce waiting time across devices.
- Enhanced sync responsiveness so clipboard history transfers more quickly and reliably.

### 📱 Mobile App Update

- A new version of the IOS app is now available with a smoother experience.
- Optimized iCloud sync speed and strategy on mobile for better performance, even on slower connections.
- Tighter integration with Mac ensures real-time consistency of clipboard history across all devices.
