## 本次更新

- 修复保留期自动清理误删自建分组内容的问题：收藏、自建分组、从 Paste 导入的 Pinboard 和多分组条目均会保留。同步更新设置说明。#58
- 改进复制图片的识别和预览：支持读取 JPEG 等剪贴板图片格式，遇到无效图片表示时继续尝试其他格式；临时图片文件不可读时，回退到复制时保留的图片数据。#57

自动清理保护适用于今后的清理，无法恢复此前已删除的记录。手动“清空历史”仍按确认框说明仅保留收藏。

## What's New

- Fixed automatic retention cleanup deleting items in custom groups. Favorites, custom groups, imported Paste pinboards, and multi-group items are now protected. Updated the settings description. #58
- Improved copied-image capture and previews: read JPEG and other advertised image formats, try alternate representations when one is invalid, and fall back to captured image data when a temporary image file cannot be read. #57

Retention protection applies to future cleanup and does not restore previously deleted records. Manually clearing history continues to keep only Favorites, as stated in its confirmation dialog.
