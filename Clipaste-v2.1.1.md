## ✨ 本次更新

### 🚀 iOS 版本正式来袭

- Clipaste iOS 版已经上架 App Store。
- 它不只是一个剪贴板 App，更是一款可以随时切换出来使用的输入法键盘。
- 在任何可以输入文字的地方，都可以切到 Clipaste，直接取用常用文本、链接和图片。
- 开启 iCloud 同步后，Mac 端的剪贴板历史可以同步到 iOS 键盘里，随时可用。

### 🛠 修复与体验优化

- 修复快捷粘贴只能使用前 9 项的问题。现在滚动到后面的内容时，也可以对当前可见内容使用快捷粘贴。
- 修复盲打搜索无法输入符号的问题。现在 `Shift + 数字` 输入的符号也可以正常触发搜索。

### ☁️ iCloud 同步优化

- 优化 iCloud 同步体验，提升多端数据同步的稳定性与一致性。
- 改进 Mac 与 iOS 之间的同步衔接，让剪贴板历史在多设备间更自然地流转。

## ✨ What's New

### 🚀 iOS Version Is Here

- Clipaste for iOS is now available on the App Store.
- It is not just a clipboard app. It is a keyboard you can switch to anytime.
- Wherever you can type, you can switch to Clipaste and quickly reuse saved text, links, and images.
- With iCloud sync enabled, your Mac clipboard history can appear in the iOS keyboard and stay available when you need it.

### 🛠 Fixes & Improvements

- Fixed quick paste only working with the first 9 items. After scrolling, quick paste now works with the currently visible items.
- Fixed type-to-search not accepting symbols. Symbols typed with `Shift + number` now correctly activate search.

### ☁️ iCloud Sync Improvements

- Improved iCloud sync stability and consistency across devices.
- Refined the connection between Mac and iOS so clipboard history moves more smoothly between them.
