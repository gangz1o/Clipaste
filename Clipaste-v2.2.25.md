## 本次更新

本版修复卡片顶部颜色取得不准、同一个 App 颜色不统一的问题，并补齐各语言的翻译。

- **卡片颜色更准确**：改进从 App 图标取色的方式，优先取图标的品牌色，不再被深色背景或阴影带偏。例如 Obsidian、Raycast、密码等 App 之前显示为黑色，现在会显示各自的品牌色；Chrome 也不会再在黄、红、绿之间随机变化。
- **同一个 App 颜色统一**：同一个 App 的所有卡片现在使用同一种颜色，旧记录也会自动生效，无需重新复制。
- **多语言补全**：补齐置顶、剪贴板变化提示音、记录其他设备内容、首次引导、分组图标选择等文案的德语、法语、日语、韩语翻译，部分缺失的中文翻译也已补上；iCloud 同步错误提示也会按系统语言显示。

## What's New

This release makes card header colors accurate and consistent, and completes translations across all languages.

- **More accurate card colors**: Colors picked from app icons now favor the icon's brand color instead of dark backgrounds or shadows. Apps such as Obsidian, Raycast and Passwords previously showed black headers and now show their brand colors, and Chrome no longer switches between yellow, red and green.
- **Consistent colors per app**: All cards from the same app now share one color, including existing history items — no need to copy them again.
- **Complete translations**: Adds German, French, Japanese and Korean translations for pinning, the clipboard change sound, content from other devices, onboarding and the group icon picker, plus a few missing Chinese strings. iCloud sync error messages now follow the system language.
